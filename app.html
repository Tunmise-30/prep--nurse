Medical-Surgical Nursing Cardiovascular
Hypertension

Learn: Hypertension = BP ≥ 140/90 mmHg on repeated checks.

Understand: Heart pumps harder against tight vessels. Kidneys hold salt and water. Risk rises with age, family history, salt, alcohol, low activity.

Apply: A patient arrives with severe headache, blurred vision, BP 190/120. The nurse checks airway, breathing, circulation first, repeats BP, and calls for urgent help — this may be hypertensive emergency.

Educational information only — not medical advice for a real patient.
